01 / What ARIA is

The institutional home for research and enterprise at IIIT Raichur.

AI Research & Innovation Arena (ARIA) is a Section 8, not-for-profit company promoted by IIIT Raichur. It functions as the institute's research park — the shared platform through which applied research, startup incubation, and industry and government partnerships are carried out.

ARIA's work is organised through specialised Centres of Excellence. The first of these, CoE-AI, focuses on artificial intelligence and is approved and funded by the Government of Karnataka. As ARIA grows, further Centres of Excellence are planned across other technology domains, each drawing on ARIA's shared governance, infrastructure and incubation support.

Our ambition

To build a self-sustaining ecosystem of research, enterprise and talent around IIIT Raichur — creating knowledge, opportunity and meaningful solutions.

02 / How we work

One connected innovation journey.

Every Centre of Excellence under ARIA follows the same integrated pathway — from foundational learning and shared infrastructure to experimentation, enterprise and wider adoption.

  1. 01

    Learn

    Practical learning through workshops, programmes and project-based experience.

  2. 02

    Build

    Computing, prototyping and collaborative environments for system development.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Challenges, mentorship and applied research to shape solutions around real needs.

  4. 04

    Scale

    Incubation, partnerships and outreach that can move strong ideas towards impact.

05 / Ecosystem

Collaboration is core infrastructure.

ARIA works with government, technology partners, industry, academia and district administrations across every centre it hosts. CoE-AI's regional AI capacity-building is approved and funded by the Government of Karnataka, with funding support from the LEAP programme; knowledge partnerships with organisations such as STPI and CySecK, and the NAIN network, extend infrastructure, mentorship and market access to founders and researchers across ARIA's network.

  • Government of Karnataka — CoE-AI
  • Local Economy Accelerator Program (LEAP)
  • New Age Incubation Network (NAIN)
  • Software Technology Parks of India (STPI)
  • Centre of Excellence in Cybersecurity (CySecK)
  • VTU & its affiliated colleges
  • District Associations
  • Deputy Commissioners (DCs)
